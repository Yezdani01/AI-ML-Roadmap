Main LangChain components

        Component	        Purpose

        Models  	        Connect to and invoke AI models
        Prompts	            Create structured instructions
        Output-Parsers	    Convert model outputs into usable formats
        Document Loaders	Load PDFs, text files, webpages, etc.
        Text Splitters	    Divide large documents into smaller chunks
        Embeddings	        Convert text into numerical vectors
        Vector Stores	    Store and search vectors
        Retrievers	        Retrieve relevant documents


        A simplified LangChain workflow

            1    Document Loader
                 Loads a PDF or text document


            2    Text Splitter
                 Splits it into smaller chunks


            3    Embeddings + Vector Store
                 Converts and stores document vectors


            4    Retriever
                 Finds relevant document chunks


            5    Prompt + LLM
                 Uses retrieved information to generate an answer
